# Architecture / Interior Studio Art Directions

## Spatial editorial

**Use when:** design-led studios

**Language:** large project imagery, asymmetric editorial grid, project metadata

## Minimal modernist

**Use when:** modern architecture practices

**Language:** precise grid, restrained palette, architectural typography

## Material cinematic

**Use when:** premium interiors

**Language:** texture, detail photography, slower spatial transitions

## Selection rule

Choose one primary direction. Add a secondary direction only if it solves a specific brand tension.
State why the chosen direction fits this business subtype and audience.

## Do not

Do not use a style simply because it is trending.
Do not default to generic gradients, cards, or cinematic motion when the domain needs utility.
