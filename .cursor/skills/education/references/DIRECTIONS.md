# Education / Learning Art Directions

## Outcome-led modern

**Use when:** bootcamps/courses

**Language:** student work and learning progression

## Institutional editorial

**Use when:** schools/institutions

**Language:** academic credibility + structured information

## Playful learning

**Use when:** younger audiences

**Language:** expressive but navigable

## Selection rule

Choose one primary direction. Add a secondary direction only if it solves a specific brand tension.
State why the chosen direction fits this business subtype and audience.

## Do not

Do not use a style simply because it is trending.
Do not default to generic gradients, cards, or cinematic motion when the domain needs utility.
