# Beauty / Wellness Domain Model

## Subtypes

- spa
- salon
- skincare
- cosmetics
- makeup studio
- wellness clinic

## Primary audiences

- service clients
- product buyers
- premium beauty customers
- wellness seekers

## Common conversions

- book treatment
- shop product
- discover service
- consult

## Trust signals

- practitioner expertise
- ingredients/treatments
- before/after only when appropriate and truthful
- real space/product imagery
- reviews

## Discovery questions for the agent

- Which subtype is this?
- Who makes the purchase/decision?
- Is the site primarily discovery, evaluation, transaction, booking, or lead generation?
- What proof exists and what must not be fabricated?
- What content/media is actually available?
- What industry cliché should be deliberately avoided?
