# Beauty / Wellness Art Directions

## Premium ritual

**Use when:** spa/skincare

**Language:** tactile macro imagery, elegant type, quiet motion

## Clinical refined

**Use when:** clinical beauty

**Language:** clean evidence-led system, controlled imagery

## Expressive beauty

**Use when:** makeup/cosmetics

**Language:** bold crop, color, typography, energetic transitions

## Selection rule

Choose one primary direction. Add a secondary direction only if it solves a specific brand tension.
State why the chosen direction fits this business subtype and audience.

## Do not

Do not use a style simply because it is trending.
Do not default to generic gradients, cards, or cinematic motion when the domain needs utility.
