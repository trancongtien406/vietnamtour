# AI Product / Startup Motion Intelligence

## Strong opportunities

- input-to-result sequence
- agent workflow states
- tool-call/data-flow visualization
- live output transitions

## Motion decision

Use motion only when it:

- reinforces the brand
- reveals a relationship or process
- preserves spatial continuity
- provides useful feedback

## Avoid

- glowing AI orb
- random particles
- fake terminal
- vague 'AI-powered' claims
- motion implying capabilities that do not exist

## Production fallback

On mobile and reduced-motion contexts, preserve the same information and narrative using a simpler vertical/static form.
