# Visual Languages

## Editorial

Use type hierarchy, captions, rules, asymmetry, image sequencing, and varying density.
Best for: architecture, culture, boutique law, fashion, premium hospitality, thought leadership.
Avoid turning every section into a “magazine” when the user needs fast task completion.

## Restrained luxury

Confidence through space, material, crop, typography, and restraint.
Luxury is not gold + black.
Use fewer elements, higher asset quality, slower rhythm.

## Technical / systemic

Use grids, diagrams, states, data, mechanism, product behavior, technical typography.
Best for SaaS, AI, engineering, fintech, infrastructure.

## Cinematic

Atmosphere through large media, sequencing, controlled reveal, spatial continuity.
Best when strong imagery exists.
Avoid forcing cinematic behavior onto dense transactional products.

## Expressive / playful

Characterful type, interaction, illustration, surprise.
Best for youth brands, creative studios, some food and entertainment.
Keep orientation and controls obvious.

## Organic / tactile

Texture, imperfect geometry, natural photography, material surfaces, gentle movement.
Best for wellness, food, craft, eco, hospitality.
