# Typography Direction

## Pairing logic

Choose pairing from brand tension, not fashion.

Serif display + sans body:

- editorial
- legal boutique
- luxury hospitality
- architecture
- fashion

Grotesk + mono accent:

- technology
- engineering
- AI
- developer portfolio

Humanist sans:

- healthcare
- education
- approachable service

Single variable family:

- clean systems
- product sites
- brands needing consistency and performance

## Hierarchy

Display is not body enlarged.
Metadata should have a distinct role.
Labels should remain legible and not be over-letterspaced.
Use line length and leading to establish reading comfort.

## Motion

Animate display type more aggressively than body.
Never make body copy wait for character animation.
