# Healthcare / Clinic Domain Model

## Subtypes

- clinic
- specialist practice
- hospital service
- health product
- therapy/service

## Primary audiences

- patients
- caregivers
- referrers
- health consumers

## Common conversions

- book appointment
- find service
- contact clinic
- understand treatment

## Trust signals

- credentials
- service details
- location/hours
- evidence-based information
- clear policies

## Discovery questions for the agent

- Which subtype is this?
- Who makes the purchase/decision?
- Is the site primarily discovery, evaluation, transaction, booking, or lead generation?
- What proof exists and what must not be fabricated?
- What content/media is actually available?
- What industry cliché should be deliberately avoided?
