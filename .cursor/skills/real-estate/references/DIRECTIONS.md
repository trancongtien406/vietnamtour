# Real Estate Art Directions

## Luxury editorial

**Use when:** luxury residential and destination property

**Language:** full-bleed photography, refined typography, generous space

## Developer cinematic

**Use when:** new developments

**Language:** masterplan/story-led chapters, architecture and amenities

## Marketplace functional

**Use when:** listing portals

**Language:** search-first UI, map/list coordination, compact but polished visual system

## Selection rule

Choose one primary direction. Add a secondary direction only if it solves a specific brand tension.
State why the chosen direction fits this business subtype and audience.

## Do not

Do not use a style simply because it is trending.
Do not default to generic gradients, cards, or cinematic motion when the domain needs utility.
