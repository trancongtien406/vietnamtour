# UI Quality Gate

Review at desktop and mobile.

## First-glance hierarchy

Can a new visitor identify:

- who this is for
- what is offered
- why it matters
- what to do next

## Art direction

- does it fit the industry and position
- is typography intentional
- does section rhythm vary
- is there a recognizable signature idea
- are image crops and media treatment coherent
- does it avoid generic template patterns

## UX

- CTA hierarchy
- navigation clarity
- form behavior
- touch behavior
- loading/error/empty states where required

## Content

- no placeholder-sounding claims
- proof is specific
- headings communicate meaning, not decoration

## Mobile

- not merely collapsed desktop
- no awkward overflow
- readable line lengths
- touch targets usable
- animation simplification intentional

Do not pass if the design is attractive but interchangeable with unrelated businesses.
