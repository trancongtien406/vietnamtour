# Visual Experience Intelligence

Visual Experience is a specialization beneath Frontend → Design Engineering. Route illustration, iconography, mascots, decorative assets, game sprites, environment assets, prompt compilation, asset manifests, visual consistency, and asset QA here.

Before generation or implementation, decompose scenes into independently usable assets and specify dimensions, format, transparency, layers, states, naming, integration constraints, and visual references. Verify outputs for resolution, crop, edges, legibility, transparency, consistency, and runtime fit.

Original source material is preserved under `.engineer-intelligence/knowledge/design-engineer-intelligence-v0.9.0/`; the integrated v0.9.1 materials are colocated under this specialization.
