# Character & Mascot Consistency

## Problem

Independent generations drift in face, proportions, hair/fur, outfit, palette,
markings, accessories, and style.

Do not manage a recurring character as a series of independent prompts.

## Pipeline

```text
Character Bible
→ master reference
→ approval
→ turnaround / pose sheet
→ expression sheet
→ production variants
```

## Generation rule

```text
IDENTITY = invariant
CHANGE = explicit delta
```

Repeat important identity invariants in generation/edit instructions.

Prefer coherent sheets for related variants:

```text
neutral | happy | worried | angry
front | back | left | right
idle | carry | work | sit
```

Review at final rendered scale.
