# Brand & Content Imagery

## Goal

Product imagery, website imagery, OpenGraph, app-store visuals, social creative,
and campaign art should share a recognizable visual DNA without requiring
identical compositions.

## Brand image grammar

Define:

- recurring subjects;
- composition tendencies;
- visual distance/intimacy;
- color relationships;
- material/texture language;
- lighting;
- emotional tone;
- cultural context;
- forbidden clichés;
- typography relationship;
- logo usage boundaries.

Avoid unrelated AI-art styles across product and marketing surfaces.
