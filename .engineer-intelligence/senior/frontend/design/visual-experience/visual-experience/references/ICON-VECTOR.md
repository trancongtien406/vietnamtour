# Icon & Vector System

## Prime rule

Use familiar icons for familiar actions.

Do not redesign search, close, back, settings, delete, edit, or share merely to
look unique.

## Use custom iconography for

- product-specific concepts;
- professions;
- game systems;
- branded categories;
- unique statuses;
- custom resource types.

## Icon vs illustration

Icon:

- reads at small size;
- simple silhouette;
- semantic;
- constrained details;
- consistent optical weight.

Illustration:

- expressive;
- larger;
- more detailed;
- identity / story / atmosphere oriented.

## Quality checks

At actual target size:

- is the metaphor recognizable?
- is it distinct from neighboring icons?
- does stroke/fill weight match?
- is the optical center correct?
- does it survive disabled/low-contrast states?
- does it rely on detail that disappears at small size?

Prefer vector for semantic icon systems and simple branded symbols.
