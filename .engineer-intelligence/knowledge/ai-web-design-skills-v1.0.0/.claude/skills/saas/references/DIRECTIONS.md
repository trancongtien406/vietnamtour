# SaaS Art Directions

## Product-system

**Use when:** most B2B SaaS

**Language:** real UI states, workflow visualization, strong information hierarchy

## Technical editorial

**Use when:** developer/infra tools

**Language:** documentation-like precision + strong narrative

## Category challenger

**Use when:** new category products

**Language:** bold positioning with evidence-led product demo

## Selection rule

Choose one primary direction. Add a secondary direction only if it solves a specific brand tension.
State why the chosen direction fits this business subtype and audience.

## Do not

Do not use a style simply because it is trending.
Do not default to generic gradients, cards, or cinematic motion when the domain needs utility.
