# Education / Learning Domain Model

## Subtypes

- school
- academy
- online course
- learning platform
- bootcamp
- training provider

## Primary audiences

- students
- parents
- professionals
- employers

## Common conversions

- enroll/apply
- explore curriculum
- book consultation
- start learning

## Trust signals

- curriculum
- instructors
- student work/outcomes
- accreditation if applicable
- clear schedule/pricing

## Discovery questions for the agent

- Which subtype is this?
- Who makes the purchase/decision?
- Is the site primarily discovery, evaluation, transaction, booking, or lead generation?
- What proof exists and what must not be fabricated?
- What content/media is actually available?
- What industry cliché should be deliberately avoided?
