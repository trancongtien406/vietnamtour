# Restaurant / Cafe / Food Art Directions

## Fine dining cinematic

**Use when:** fine dining

**Language:** sensory photography, refined type, slow restrained reveal

## Warm editorial

**Use when:** cafes and bakeries

**Language:** human photography, tactile surfaces, friendly typography

## Bold product-forward

**Use when:** fast-service

**Language:** high contrast, fast rhythm, clear product focus

## Selection rule

Choose one primary direction. Add a secondary direction only if it solves a specific brand tension.
State why the chosen direction fits this business subtype and audience.

## Do not

Do not use a style simply because it is trending.
Do not default to generic gradients, cards, or cinematic motion when the domain needs utility.
