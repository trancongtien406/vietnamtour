# Restaurant / Cafe / Food Domain Model

## Subtypes

- fine dining
- casual restaurant
- cafe
- bakery
- fast-service
- experiential dining

## Primary audiences

- local diners
- tourists
- regulars
- event/group guests

## Common conversions

- view menu
- reserve table
- order
- get directions
- call

## Trust signals

- real food/space photography
- hours/location
- menu/pricing
- reservation clarity
- reviews/press when verifiable

## Discovery questions for the agent

- Which subtype is this?
- Who makes the purchase/decision?
- Is the site primarily discovery, evaluation, transaction, booking, or lead generation?
- What proof exists and what must not be fabricated?
- What content/media is actually available?
- What industry cliché should be deliberately avoided?
