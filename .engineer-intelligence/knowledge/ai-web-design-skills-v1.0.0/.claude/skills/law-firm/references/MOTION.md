# Law Firm / Legal Services Motion Intelligence

## Strong opportunities

- line and image mask reveals
- attorney/profile transitions
- editorial index hover states
- restrained section transitions

## Motion decision

Use motion only when it:

- reinforces the brand
- reveals a relationship or process
- preserves spatial continuity
- provides useful feedback

## Avoid

- playful cursor gimmicks
- aggressive neon
- scramble text on important content
- overly cinematic intro before contact information
- generic scales-of-justice stock imagery

## Production fallback

On mobile and reduced-motion contexts, preserve the same information and narrative using a simpler vertical/static form.
