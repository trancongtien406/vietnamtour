# Law Firm / Legal Services Art Directions

## Boutique editorial

**Use when:** boutique, private-client, premium advisory

**Language:** refined serif + neutral sans, strong editorial spacing, discreet imagery, expert-led narrative

## Institutional modern

**Use when:** corporate, cross-border, larger practice

**Language:** structured grid, strong typography, evidence-led pages, restrained motion

## Technology legal

**Use when:** IP, startup, privacy, technology law

**Language:** precise grotesk, technical diagrams, clean data-like interactions

## Selection rule

Choose one primary direction. Add a secondary direction only if it solves a specific brand tension.
State why the chosen direction fits this business subtype and audience.

## Do not

Do not use a style simply because it is trending.
Do not default to generic gradients, cards, or cinematic motion when the domain needs utility.
