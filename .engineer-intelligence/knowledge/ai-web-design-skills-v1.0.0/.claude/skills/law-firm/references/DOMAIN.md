# Law Firm / Legal Services Domain Model

## Subtypes

- corporate law
- boutique practice
- litigation
- family law
- criminal defense
- IP / technology law

## Primary audiences

- business owners and executives
- individual clients under stress
- in-house legal teams
- international clients

## Common conversions

- book consultation
- call / contact counsel
- submit matter details
- read expertise and attorney profiles

## Trust signals

- attorney credentials
- jurisdictions
- practice expertise
- representative matters where legally appropriate
- awards/directories when verifiable
- clear confidentiality language

## Discovery questions for the agent

- Which subtype is this?
- Who makes the purchase/decision?
- Is the site primarily discovery, evaluation, transaction, booking, or lead generation?
- What proof exists and what must not be fabricated?
- What content/media is actually available?
- What industry cliché should be deliberately avoided?
