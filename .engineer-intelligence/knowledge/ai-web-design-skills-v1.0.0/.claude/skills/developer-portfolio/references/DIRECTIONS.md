# Developer / Engineer Portfolio Art Directions

## Technical editorial

**Use when:** senior/product engineers

**Language:** case studies + systems thinking

## Experimental interactive

**Use when:** frontend/creative dev

**Language:** selective motion/technical experiments

## Product engineering studio

**Use when:** freelancers/studios

**Language:** business outcomes + engineering depth

## Selection rule

Choose one primary direction. Add a secondary direction only if it solves a specific brand tension.
State why the chosen direction fits this business subtype and audience.

## Do not

Do not use a style simply because it is trending.
Do not default to generic gradients, cards, or cinematic motion when the domain needs utility.
