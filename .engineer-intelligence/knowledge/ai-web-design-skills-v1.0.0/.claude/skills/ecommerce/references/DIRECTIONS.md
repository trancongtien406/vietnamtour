# E-commerce / DTC Art Directions

## Editorial commerce

**Use when:** fashion/lifestyle

**Language:** brand storytelling + strong merchandising

## Product macro

**Use when:** premium physical goods

**Language:** detail-led visuals and material storytelling

## High-clarity retail

**Use when:** large catalogs

**Language:** fast category/product browsing

## Selection rule

Choose one primary direction. Add a secondary direction only if it solves a specific brand tension.
State why the chosen direction fits this business subtype and audience.

## Do not

Do not use a style simply because it is trending.
Do not default to generic gradients, cards, or cinematic motion when the domain needs utility.
