# Healthcare / Clinic Art Directions

## Calm clinical

**Use when:** clinics

**Language:** clear hierarchy, human imagery, quiet system

## Human-centered

**Use when:** family/community services

**Language:** warm photography, approachable language

## Modern specialist

**Use when:** specialist practices

**Language:** precise technical presentation, restrained premium cues

## Selection rule

Choose one primary direction. Add a secondary direction only if it solves a specific brand tension.
State why the chosen direction fits this business subtype and audience.

## Do not

Do not use a style simply because it is trending.
Do not default to generic gradients, cards, or cinematic motion when the domain needs utility.
