# Hotel / Resort / Hospitality Art Directions

## Destination cinematic

**Use when:** resorts/villas

**Language:** large place-led media and chapter storytelling

## Boutique editorial

**Use when:** boutique hotels

**Language:** human scale, local story, refined type

## Modern hospitality

**Use when:** urban hotels

**Language:** clean booking-led design with premium imagery

## Selection rule

Choose one primary direction. Add a secondary direction only if it solves a specific brand tension.
State why the chosen direction fits this business subtype and audience.

## Do not

Do not use a style simply because it is trending.
Do not default to generic gradients, cards, or cinematic motion when the domain needs utility.
