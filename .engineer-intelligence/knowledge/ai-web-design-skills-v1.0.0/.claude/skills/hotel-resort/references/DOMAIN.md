# Hotel / Resort / Hospitality Domain Model

## Subtypes

- city hotel
- luxury resort
- boutique hotel
- villa
- wellness retreat

## Primary audiences

- leisure travelers
- couples/families
- business travelers
- international guests

## Common conversions

- check availability
- book room
- explore rooms
- explore experiences

## Trust signals

- real room/property imagery
- amenities
- location
- policies
- reviews/awards when verifiable

## Discovery questions for the agent

- Which subtype is this?
- Who makes the purchase/decision?
- Is the site primarily discovery, evaluation, transaction, booking, or lead generation?
- What proof exists and what must not be fabricated?
- What content/media is actually available?
- What industry cliché should be deliberately avoided?
