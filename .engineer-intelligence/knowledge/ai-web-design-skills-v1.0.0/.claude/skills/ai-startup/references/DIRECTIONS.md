# AI Product / Startup Art Directions

## Capability-first

**Use when:** AI products with visible workflow

**Language:** show input → reasoning/action → result

## Technical system

**Use when:** developer and infrastructure AI

**Language:** architecture/data flow/evaluation driven

## Creative output-led

**Use when:** media/generative tools

**Language:** generated results become the art direction

## Selection rule

Choose one primary direction. Add a secondary direction only if it solves a specific brand tension.
State why the chosen direction fits this business subtype and audience.

## Do not

Do not use a style simply because it is trending.
Do not default to generic gradients, cards, or cinematic motion when the domain needs utility.
