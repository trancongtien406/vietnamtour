# Art Direction

Define a thesis using feeling, tension, material/visual metaphor, hierarchy, imagery role, typography behavior, geometry, density and motion character.

Do not select a trend as an art direction. “Glassmorphism”, “bento”, “dark mode” or “GSAP” are techniques, not a product thesis.
