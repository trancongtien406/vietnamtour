# Design Handoff

Document:

- shell/region ownership;
- layout constraints and tokens;
- component anatomy and states;
- interaction/state transitions;
- responsive transformations;
- accessibility/keyboard/focus behavior;
- loading/empty/error/permission cases;
- motion intent and reduced-motion behavior;
- implementation risks and acceptance criteria.

Do not hand off screenshots without behavioral rules.
