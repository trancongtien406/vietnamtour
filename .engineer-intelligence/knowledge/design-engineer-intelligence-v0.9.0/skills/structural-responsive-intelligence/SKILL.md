# Structural & Responsive Intelligence

Treat structure as a hard quality layer independent from visual style.

For substantial app surfaces:

1. identify shell regions and ownership;
2. identify navigation states;
3. identify scroll ownership;
4. identify responsive transformations per region;
5. identify accessibility/keyboard behavior;
6. verify content adaptation, not only shell adaptation;
7. fail the design gate on structural errors.

Read `../../references/structural-invariants.md`, `../../references/admin-shell-invariants.md`, and `../../references/responsive-state-model.md`.
